About Rosslyn Foundation

Building a community where everyone can live with dignity.

We believe everyone deserves to feel valued, supported, and cared for, regardless of their circumstances. Through compassionate assistance and community support, we aim to help people overcome challenges and live healthier, more secure lives.

Our promise

Compassion in action. Dignity for everyone.

Our work goes beyond meeting immediate needs. We aim to help people feel seen, heard, and respected while connecting them with appropriate resources and meaningful community support. We approach every request with care and seek to make support easier to understand and access.

What we do

Practical support for life's challenges.

We focus on helping people navigate difficult circumstances, access essential resources, and feel more connected to their communities.

Our approach

Understand needs. Respond with care.

Every person's circumstances are different. We believe support should begin with listening and be guided by fairness, respect, and responsibility.

01 / LISTEN

Start with understanding

We seek to understand each person's situation without judgement and treat every request with consideration.

02 / RESPOND

Assess needs fairly

We aim to direct available resources responsibly and assess requests against relevant programme requirements.

03 / CONNECT

Build useful connections

Where appropriate, we help people explore resources, services, and community connections that may be useful.

People first

Every person has a story.

Behind every request for support is a person with experiences, aspirations, challenges, and hopes for the future. We recognise the strength it can take to ask for help, and believe that seeking assistance should never mean losing one's dignity.

Our commitment is to listen with respect, consider each person's circumstances, and help them explore appropriate support options.

Our commitment

Support built on trust and respect.

We aim to approach support requests with fairness, care, and respect for privacy. Information submitted through our website should be handled responsibly and used for appropriate purposes related to assessing or providing assistance.

We also aim to communicate clearly about programme requirements and available support. Where a request falls outside our current programmes, we will endeavour to share useful guidance or information about other resources where possible.

Eligibility and availability: Assistance depends on the foundation's actual programme requirements, geographical coverage, and available resources. Submitting a request does not guarantee approval.
