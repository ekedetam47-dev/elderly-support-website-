Before you apply

Who can request support?

You can contact Rosslyn Foundation about support for yourself, a family member, or an older adult in your care.

  • ✓Minimum age: 40 years
    The person who needs support must be at least 40 years old on the date the request is submitted.
  • ✓Resident or connected to the area
    Support is usually focused on people living in or connected to the local community served by the foundation.
  • ✓Request made by a responsible adult
    If someone is unable to ask for help themselves, another adult can submit on their behalf.

Helpful information

Frequently asked questions

Does submitting a request mean I will receive support?

No. Every request needs to be reviewed. Support depends on actual programmes, eligibility requirements, and availability.

Can I submit a request for a parent or another older adult?

Yes, you can enquire on behalf of someone else. Where appropriate, make sure you have permission to share personal details.

How long does the review take?

The foundation should publish its review timeframe. Do not assume a request has been received until the live form confirms it through a successful submission.

What if I am not sure which support category to choose?

Select “General guidance / not sure” in the form and describe your situation in your own words.

Is my information stored when I submit this form?

Forms are usually processed securely and only used for the purpose of reviewing and responding to the support request.
