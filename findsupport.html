Ways we may help

Discover the kinds of help you can ask Rosslyn Foundation about. Every person's situation is different, so share what is happening and the team can review your request against the programmes and resources available.

Care and well-being

Growing older can bring new day-to-day challenges. You can ask about practical support that may help an older adult maintain comfort, dignity, independence, and well-being. Tell us which daily activities or care-related needs are becoming difficult so your situation can be understood.

▤

Food and essential supplies

When it becomes difficult to afford everyday essentials, the pressure can affect the whole household. You can describe a need for food, basic household supplies, or other essential items. Include a brief explanation of the difficulty so the team can assess whether a suitable programme or referral may be available.

＋

Health-related needs

Health concerns can create additional practical and financial strain for older adults and their families. Explain the type of support you are seeking, such as help navigating available community resources or asking about a possible referral. Rosslyn Foundation does not replace medical professionals or emergency services.

◎

Companionship and community

Connection and a sense of belonging matter at every age. You can ask about community activities, opportunities to connect with others, or support for an older adult who may be feeling isolated. Share what kind of social or community connection would be most helpful.

$

Financial hardship

Unexpected expenses or limited income can make everyday living more difficult. You can explain the general financial challenge and how it affects the older adult's essential needs. Please do not include bank account numbers, card details, passwords, or PINs. Any assistance depends on programme criteria and available resources.

☰

Guidance with finding support

You do not need to know the exact name of a service before reaching out. If you are unsure where to start, describe the situation in your own words and what would make things easier. The team can review your enquiry and explain any relevant options or next steps that may be available.

Before you apply

Who can request support?

You can contact Rosslyn Foundation about your own needs or on behalf of an older adult, where appropriate.

  • ✓Minimum age: 40 years
    The person who needs support must be at least 40 years old on the date the request is submitted.
  • ✓Location
    Applicants must live in an area currently served by the Rosslyn Foundation. The team may ask for their town or city to confirm coverage.
  • ✓Demonstrated need
    Requests may relate to essential supplies, well-being, health-related needs, financial hardship, companionship, or guidance.
  • ✓Review and available resources
    Requests are reviewed respectfully. Approval depends on programme availability, the information provided, and available resources. Meeting the criteria does not guarantee assistance.
Important: A family member or caregiver may submit a request on behalf of someone aged 40 or older. The foundation may ask for additional information to assess the request. Please provide accurate details; submitting a request does not guarantee support.

Helpful information

Frequently asked questions

Does submitting a request mean I will receive support?

No. Every request needs to be reviewed. Support depends on actual programmes, eligibility requirements, and available resources.

Can I submit a request for a parent or another older adult?

Yes, you can enquire on behalf of someone else. Where appropriate, make sure you have permission to share their information and provide only what is necessary.

How long does the review take?

The foundation should publish its review timeframe. Do not assume a request has been received until the live form confirms it through a connected backend.

What if I am not sure which support category to choose?

Select “General guidance / not sure” in the form and describe your situation in your own words.
