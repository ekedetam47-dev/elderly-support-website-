Make a meaningful difference

Give Your Time. Share Your Care.

At Rosslyn Foundation, volunteers help us support older adults with compassion, dignity, and respect. Your time, skills, and kindness can make a real difference in someone’s day.

Whether you enjoy spending time with people, helping with community activities, sharing professional skills, or supporting outreach work, there may be a place for you on our team.

Every act of kindness matters. Explore the opportunities below and use the application form when you’re ready to get involved.

How to Volunteer

Getting involved starts with three simple steps.

1

Explore the Roles

Review the opportunities above and think about the skills, interests, and time you can offer.

2

Send Your Application

Complete the volunteer application form with your contact details and preferred area of support.

3

Connect With Our Team

Our team can review your application and explain suitable next steps, subject to the foundation’s process.
